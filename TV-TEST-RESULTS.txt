Compatibility and limits

The code targets webOS TV 4.0 and later. The exact LG model has not yet been
provided. ES5 syntax and supported platform APIs guide compatibility, but are
not equivalent to an installation/decoding test on every LG generation.
Progress remains local to the television. No transcoding or media downloads
occur on the TV. Developer Mode session expiry removes sideloaded apps after
a subsequent restart. Renew the session through LG's EXTEND control.
